# Daily Codebase Improvement Log: 2026-06-12

## 💡 What I Discovered & Idea Generated
**What:** The `_perform_artifact_chaining` method in `detectivejoe.py` was iterating through targets and sequentially awaiting each `self.worker_pool.execute_plugin_batch` execution in a `for` loop.
**Why:** This led to poor performance because multiple targets were processed one after another instead of concurrently.
**Impact:** Sequential chaining significantly slowed down artifact execution times when a large number of targets were discovered.
**Measurement:** Concurrent execution through `asyncio.gather` drastically reduces the overall duration of the chaining phase.

## 🛠️ What I Fixed / Added
Refactored the `_perform_artifact_chaining` method to build a list of plugin coroutines and then executed them all concurrently using `asyncio.gather(*coroutines, return_exceptions=True)`. Processed the gathered results by matching them to their target and updating `chained_results`. Handled exceptions securely for individually failed chained tasks without interrupting the entire batch.

## 🎯 Next Recommended Target
I recommend looking into evaluating caching or duplicate detection mechanisms before enqueuing chained artifacts, to further optimize performance.
