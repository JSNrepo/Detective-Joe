What I discovered:
The codebase was using a full sort `sorted()` in `intelligence.py` to retrieve the 10 most recent artifacts out of potentially many, resulting in O(N log N) performance.

What I fixed/added:
Replaced the full sort with `heapq.nlargest` (adding `import heapq`) to improve performance from O(N log N) to O(N log K), alongside adding comments to explain the optimization.
